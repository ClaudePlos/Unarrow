Unarrow

The board is jammed with arrows. Your job is to remove them all.

  • Click an arrow — it slides out along its own track, off the board.
  • That only works if the straight path ahead of its head, all the way to the edge, is completely clear.
  • A blocked arrow shakes red. Clear whatever is in its way first.
  • Every level can be finished — the layout can never lock you out.

Shortcuts: H hint, R restart.
